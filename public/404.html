Install Smart Finance Calculator

It opens like an app, works offline, and takes almost no space.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the three-dot menu at the top right.
  2. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install.

Not in the menu yet? Open a couple of pages first. Chrome offers installation after a short while, and after clearing site data it can take a few minutes to come back.

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.

This only works in Safari, not inside other apps' browsers.

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  2. Or open the browser menu and choose Install Smart Finance Calculator.
