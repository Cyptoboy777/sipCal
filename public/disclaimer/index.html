Calculator results are estimates based on the values and assumptions you enter. In particular:

  • Investments. Returns on mutual funds and other market-linked investments are not guaranteed and can be negative. A SIP result shows what would happen at the return you type in; it is not a forecast. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Read all scheme-related documents carefully. Past performance is not an indicator of future returns.
