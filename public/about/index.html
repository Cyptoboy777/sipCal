About this site

Smart Finance Calculator is a free set of money calculators for people in India, with the working shown.

What this site is for

Most people meet big financial numbers in a hurry: a loan offer with an EMI printed on it, a SIP projection in a brochure, a deposit rate on a bank poster. The figure is rarely explained. We built these calculators so you can see how the number is made, change the assumptions, and read the full schedule behind it, in rupees and in the lakh and crore format you already think in.

What you will find here

  • EMI calculator for home, car and personal loans, with prepayment and a full repayment schedule.
  • SIP calculator with lump sum, yearly step-up and inflation-adjusted results.
  • Compound interest calculator with different compounding frequencies and monthly deposits.
  • FD calculator for cumulative fixed deposits.
  • Guides that explain the ideas, with worked numbers produced by the same code as the calculators.

How we keep the numbers right

Each calculator uses the standard formula for its purpose, and every page spells out that formula and its assumptions. Before changes go live, the calculation engine is checked against independent calculations written separately from the engine: closed-form loan and future-value formulas, a loan schedule that must end at exactly zero, and principal repaid that must add up to the amount borrowed. Every worked example in our articles is generated from the engine, so an article cannot quote a number the calculator would not produce.

We are open about what the tools do not know. They do not know your lender’s fees, your tax position or what a market will return. Each page says what is left out.

Editorial independence

We do not sell loans, funds, deposits or insurance, and we do not take commissions or referral fees from any lender, bank or fund house. We do not recommend a particular product. The site is funded by advertising, including ads served by Google AdSense, which appear separately from our content and have no influence on what we write or how we calculate.

Corrections and updates

Tax rules, deposit limits and regulations change. Each page shows the date it was last reviewed, and when we learn something is out of date we update it. If you spot an error in a calculation or an article, please tell us. We read every message about accuracy.

Install Smart Finance Calculator

It opens like an app, works offline, and takes almost no space.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the three-dot menu at the top right.
  2. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install.

Not in the menu yet? Open a couple of pages first. Chrome offers installation after a short while, and after clearing site data it can take a few minutes to come back.

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.

This only works in Safari, not inside other apps' browsers.

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  2. Or open the browser menu and choose Install Smart Finance Calculator.
