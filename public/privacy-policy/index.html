Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026.

The short version

The numbers you type into our calculators are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. We do not ask you to create an account. The site is supported by advertising, and the advertising partners we use, including Google, may use cookies as described below.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect the loan amounts, investment amounts, rates or any other values you enter into the calculators. They are calculated on your device. We do not require your name, phone number or email address to use any calculator.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider receives standard technical information when your browser requests a page, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the page requested and the time. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and understand overall usage. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Links that contain your numbers

The Copy link button creates a web address that includes the values you entered, so you can share them. Those values live in the link itself. Only people you give the link to can see them. Be mindful when pasting such a link in a public place.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies, including web beacons and device identifiers, to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, to measure ads, and to prevent fraud.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can manage personalised advertising for your Google account at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

Where required by law, such as for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, consent for personalised advertising is requested through a consent message, and you can change your choice at any time.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Doing so does not stop the calculators from working, but you may see ads that are less relevant to you.

Cookies and storage we set

The calculators themselves do not set cookies and do not store your inputs. Cookies on this site come from advertising and measurement partners, as described above.

Third-party links

Our pages link to other sites, such as government and regulator pages. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Children

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you beyond what is described above, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. If you have emailed us and want your message deleted, or you have any question about how your information is handled, contact us at cryptovarma4@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or other applicable data protection laws.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Please check it from time to time.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to cryptovarma4@gmail.com, or see our contact page.
