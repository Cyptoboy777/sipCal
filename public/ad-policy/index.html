Ad policy

How ads are shown on this site, and what we will not accept.

How ads are shown

  • Every ad is labelled Advertisement (served by Google) or Sponsored (sold directly).
  • Ads are kept apart from content. They never sit inside a calculator, its inputs, its results or its schedule tables, and they do not appear on this policy, privacy, terms, disclaimer or contact pages.
  • Ads do not change the numbers. Advertisers cannot influence a calculation, a formula, an example or what we write in a guide.
  • We do not show pop-ups, interstitials or ads that cover the page.

What we do not accept

  • Claims of guaranteed, assured or risk-free returns on market-linked products.
  • Ads for investment advice or portfolio management from anyone who is not registered with the relevant regulator.
  • Unlicensed lending, predatory or misleading loan offers, and loan apps that hide fees or interest rates.
  • Get-rich-quick schemes, unregulated crypto or trading tips, binary options, and gambling.
  • Misleading, fake-urgency or fake-testimonial creative, and anything that imitates our site or a bank.
  • Adult, illegal or hateful content.

What advertisers must include

  • Required statements, for example “Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Read all scheme related documents carefully.”
  • Honest descriptions of rates, fees and eligibility, with a landing page that matches the ad.
  • Regulatory registration where the law requires one. The advertiser is responsible for complying with financial-services and advertising rules in every country where the ad is shown.

Our independence

We do not recommend any advertised product, and an ad is not an endorsement. Always read the terms of any product yourself and, for investments, consult a registered adviser. See the disclaimer.

Report an ad

If an ad looks misleading or inappropriate, email cryptovarma4@gmail.com with the page and a description. We review reports and remove ads that break this policy. Google ads can also be reported using the controls in the ad itself.

Install Smart Finance Calculator

It opens like an app, works offline, and takes almost no space.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the three-dot menu at the top right.
  2. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install.

Not in the menu yet? Open a couple of pages first. Chrome offers installation after a short while, and after clearing site data it can take a few minutes to come back.

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.

This only works in Safari, not inside other apps' browsers.

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  2. Or open the browser menu and choose Install Smart Finance Calculator.
